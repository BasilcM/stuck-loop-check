The Stuck Loop Check

Ten quick questions about how you start, focus, recharge and talk to yourself. You'll get your score, the one area to focus on first, and what to do about it this week.

Takes about 3 minutes. Answer for the last two weeks. The questions change each time, so you can retake it later and compare.

This is a self-reflection tool, not a medical or psychological test. No name or email is asked. Your anonymous answers and score are saved so the author can improve this check.
